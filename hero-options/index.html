Attention to Detail

Five openers for the interior pages.

The home page keeps the hero it has. This is the other four — services, gallery, about, contact — which all currently open the same way: ink band, pill, two-tone headline, cream card. Each frame below shows the services page with a different opener and the real section underneath it, so you can see how each one hands off to the content.

01

Photo band

A photograph behind the opener with an ink scrim over it, so the page starts with the work rather than with a colour. Closest to how the home page opens, and the only one that puts an image above the fold on every page.

Open full screen →
02

Cream open

The interior pages open light instead of dark, and the ink is spent once mid-page where it still counts. Makes the four pages feel like the same document as the home page rather than a separate dark section of the site.

Open full screen →
03

Hairline

No pill and no card. A numbered rule, the headline at full size, one line, one link. The quickest to the content and the least like a brochure cover — but it drops the spec card, so the facts move down the page.

Open full screen →
04

Ticket

The whole opener is one cream card on the ink: page name, price, headline and three facts. The most information above the fold, and the strongest contrast on the four pages — it reads as a receipt for what the page is about.

Open full screen →
05

Big type

Type and nothing else — a kicker, the headline at the largest size anywhere on the site, one line under it, then straight into the page. The most confident and the least helpful: no photo, no card, no numbers.
