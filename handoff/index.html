Quote requests

How a customer reaches you

Someone fills out the form on your contact page. It arrives in your Gmail within a minute, with their name, phone, email, vehicle, address, the add-ons they picked, and whatever they wrote in the notes.

If you're expecting one and it hasn't shown up

  • Check your spam folder first. The first one or two sometimes land there — mark it "not spam" once and Gmail learns.
  • Send yourself a test through the form on your own site. If your test arrives, the form is fine.
  • If your test doesn't arrive, text me. Don't wait on it.

Reply from your Gmail as normal — it goes straight back to the customer. Fastest reply usually wins this kind of work, so it's worth having notifications on for that inbox.

Changes

Getting something updated

  1. Text or email me what you want changed. Be as specific as you can — "change the interior price from $150 to $175" beats "the pricing is off."

  2. Send photos as the original files, straight from your phone's camera roll. Not screenshots, and not pasted into a text document — those come through blurry and I can't fix that on my end.

  3. If it's outside what's included, I tell you the cost before I do anything. You will never get an invoice for something you didn't agree to first.

  4. I make the change and it's live. Small things are usually same day.

Included versus new work

A revision is a change to what's already there — text, prices, photos, hours, the order of a section, a new service added to the list.

New work is a second separate website, a redesign of something already built and approved, or a feature that wasn't in the package. That gets quoted first, at your 30% discount.

Troubleshooting

If something looks wrong

  • A change you asked for isn't showing. Your phone is showing you the old saved copy. Pull down to refresh, or close the tab and reopen the site.
  • The site won't load at all. Try it on another phone or on data instead of wifi. If it's down for both, text me — it's mine to fix, not yours.
  • A photo looks stretched or wrong. Screenshot it, send it, tell me which page. That's a two-minute fix.
  • Someone tells you they couldn't submit the form. Tell me the same day. That's the one thing on the site that costs you money when it breaks.
