How I clean

Steam does most of the work.

Heavy chemicals are the quick way to clean an interior, and the quick way to wear one out.

I use as little product as a car needs and let steam do the rest. Steam pulls dirt up out of fabric, gets into vents, seams and seat rails, and does it without soaking your upholstery or leaving a chemical smell behind, so leather, cloth and plastic all come out clean instead of stripped.

Before you book

Questions I get asked.

How long does it take?

Two to three hours for most cars. A three-row, heavy dog hair, or a car that's had a rough year takes longer, and I'll tell you how much longer when I confirm the time, so you can plan the day around it.

Do I need to be home while you work?

No. If I can reach the car and get into it, you can be at work, asleep or out; plenty of people leave the keys and come back to it done. I turn up with my own power and water, so there's nothing to plug me into and nothing to lend me.

How do I pay, and when?

Cash, Venmo or Zelle, once the car is done and you've had a look at it. Nothing is due when you book and I don't take a deposit. Cards are the one thing I can't take, so it's worth sorting before I arrive.

Do you wash the outside too?

No, interiors only. Most places hurry through the inside on the way to the paint, and the inside is the part you actually sit in.

What if the car is worse than I described?

Then the price moves, and you see the new number in writing before anything is booked. I'd rather have that conversation over a message than in your driveway with the vacuum already running.

Do you come to my town?

Anywhere in Pierce County: Bonney Lake, Sumner, Puyallup, Tacoma, Orting, Buckley and Enumclaw are the usual ones. If you're a reasonable drive past the county line, ask anyway. It's usually a yes.

How soon can you get to me?

Send the form and I'll come back within 24 hours with a confirmation and a final price. If you're in a hurry, call instead. I'm on the phone from 8am to 6pm, seven days a week, and I can usually tell you on the spot whether a day is open.
