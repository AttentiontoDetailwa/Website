The person doing the work

You'll always know who's been in your car.

When you book, you get me. More than a hundred cars now, all of them start to finish. Nobody to pass you along to, and one person to tell if something isn't right.

I'm a college student and basketball player, and both have taught me a lot about showing up, working hard, and giving something my best even when no one is watching. I bring that same mindset to every car I detail.

I genuinely care about the little things. The seams, the rails, under the seats, the vents: the places that are easy to miss but make a big difference.

I know everyone's car is a little different, and sometimes a detail takes longer than expected. When that happens, I'd rather stay a little later and make sure I'm proud of the work I'm leaving you with.
