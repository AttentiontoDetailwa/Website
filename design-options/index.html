Attention to Detail

Five homepage directions.

Same business, same photos, same teal. Different layout, mood and personality. Open each, scroll it top to bottom, then tell me which one feels like Ava's business — or which pieces you want pulled from each.

01

Night Shift

Dark, premium, car-magazine. Teal is the only bright thing on the page. Split hero with a tall photo, pricing as a clean table instead of cards.

 → 02

Teal Field

Teal is the whole page, not an accent. Heavy type, staggered photos, rounded pills. The loudest and most memorable of the five.

 → 03

Full Bleed

Photo-led and quiet. Full-screen hero shot, serif headlines, lots of white space. The most upmarket, closest to a boutique studio site.

 → 04

Swiss Sand

Keeps your existing sand palette but goes editorial — numbered sections, condensed uppercase type, hairline grid, no shadows anywhere.

 → 05

Driveway

Warm and friendly. Soft cards, rounded buttons, a before/after toggle in the hero and a sticky call bar on phones. Built to convert on mobile.
