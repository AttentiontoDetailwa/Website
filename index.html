How I clean

Steam, not a shelf of chemicals.

Most interiors get cleaned with whatever lifts the stain fastest. That's also what fades a dash and leaves seats stiff.

I use as little product as a car actually needs and let steam do the rest. It pulls dirt up out of fabric and gets into vents, seams and seat rails without soaking anything. Safe on leather, cloth and every plastic surface in there. Steam is in every detail I do, and it never costs extra.
